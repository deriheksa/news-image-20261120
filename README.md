A newspaper front page that sums up the world's news of Friday 20
November 2026 in one image: 44 stories across 6 regions — 15 cards
(4 heroes + 11 grid stories) and 29 briefs. Light broadsheet on
cream paper, giant black serif masthead, a geographic region bar
(west to east), a six-number "day in numbers" strip, a small world
map with numbered pins and dots, and a date-check strip listing the
previous day's stories that were deliberately left out. Every story
happened or was first reported on 20 November 2026; disputed claims
are attributed. Submitted to
[news-image-20261120](https://make.fun/bounty/news-image-20261120) on
make.fun.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```

`main.py` is standard library only and copies the pre-rendered
`entry.jpg` to `/output/entry.png` (1024x1024, under 1 MiB).
